## Intended use

Research and education: demonstrating self-supervised pretraining, evaluation and explainability for multi-label chest X-ray classification. Outputs are model scores, not diagnoses.

**Not intended for:**

- Clinical diagnosis, triage, screening or any patient-care decision.
- Pediatric images, lateral views, non-chest images, or non-radiograph modalities.
- Populations or equipment that differ from the NIH Clinical Center 1992-2015 archive without separate validation.

## Data

- **Training:** NIH ChestX-ray14 (112,120 frontal radiographs of 30,805 patients, NIH Clinical Center). Labels for 14 findings were text-mined from radiology reports and are noisy. Trained on the official train_val list with a patient-level validation split.
- **Evaluation:** NIH official test list (25,596 images, 2,797 patients), patient-level bootstrap CIs. External: CheXpert radiologist-labeled sets where available (see external results).

## Limitations

- Training labels come from NLP on reports, not image review; measured performance is agreement with those noisy labels.
- Single-institution training data; performance drops under domain shift (see external validation).
- Heatmaps show where evidence for a score is located in the model's features; they are coarse and are not segmentations.
- The plausibility check flags images that look unlike the training data, but it cannot catch every inappropriate input.
- Probabilities are calibrated on the NIH validation split; calibration may not transfer to other sites.
